# Legal Review & Operational Pending Items

**Platform:** MetaGreenWave  
**Operator:** Digi8 Solutions  
**Document Purpose:** Actionable Legal & Compliance Punch-List for Business Owner / Legal Counsel  

---

## 1. Overview

While the technical implementation, cryptographic protections, data deletion portal, and baseline legal policies are fully deployed and tested, formal commercial operation requires finalizing specific jurisdictional, corporate, and statutory particulars with licensed legal counsel.

This document identifies items that should be reviewed and customized prior to commercial customer onboarding.

---

## 2. Actionable Legal Review Punch-List

### 2.1 Jurisdiction & Governing Law
- [ ] **Specify State / City Jurisdiction:** In `TermsOfServicePage.tsx` §8, designate the specific jurisdiction and court venue for commercial dispute resolution (e.g. "Courts of Bengaluru, Karnataka, India" or preferred jurisdiction).
- [ ] **Arbitration Clause:** Consider adding a mandatory dispute resolution and arbitration clause under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 (or regional equivalent).

### 2.2 Statutory Corporate Particulars
- [ ] **Corporate Registration Details:** Once official incorporation/registration documents are finalized, incorporate the official registered address and identification number (e.g. CIN / LLPIN / MSME Udyam ID) into `DIGI8_SOLUTIONS_BUSINESS_INFORMATION.md` and the public footer.
- [ ] **Goods and Services Tax (GST):** If registered under Indian GST:
  - Add Digi8 Solutions' GSTIN to tax invoice templates generated by `server/src/routes/billing.routes.ts`.
  - Configure applicable state CGST/SGST/IGST tax rates for domestic subscribers.

### 2.3 Data Protection Officer (DPO) Designation
- [ ] **DPO Contact Name:** Under India's DPDPA 2023 (§8(5)) and GDPR Article 37, designate a named Data Protection Officer or Grievance Officer and update `PrivacyPolicyPage.tsx` with their direct contact information.
- [ ] **Grievance Redressal Timeline:** Ensure internal operational procedures can resolve data subject grievances within the 30-day statutory timeline.

### 2.4 Customer Master Services Agreement (MSA) for Enterprise Clients
- [ ] **Custom Enterprise MSA:** For enterprise tier clients requiring customized Service Level Agreements (SLAs), dedicated database instances, or custom indemnification, develop a standardized offline Enterprise Order Form & MSA referencing platform terms.

### 2.5 Meta App Review Submission & Verification
- [ ] **Upload Official Business Verification Documents:** In Meta Business Portfolio (Settings &gt; Security Center), initiate Business Verification by uploading official Digi8 Solutions registration documents and bank statements.
- [ ] **Submit Meta App Review:** Following the instructions in `META_APP_REVIEW_GUIDE.md`, submit `whatsapp_business_management` and `whatsapp_business_messaging` permissions with the recorded walkthrough videos.
